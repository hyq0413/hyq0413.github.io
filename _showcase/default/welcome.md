<div class="p-4">
    <h2>Welcome to PIC Lab!</h2>
    <hr />
    <p>
        <code>Perception and Intelligent Computing (PIC) Lab</code> explores emerging sensing devices, novel perception modalities, and intelligent computing techniques to understand and reconstruct the physical world. We aim to build a comprehensive digital representation of the physical world through next-generation sensing(vision, tactile, etc), 3D/4D perception, multimodal intelligence, and computational imaging.
    </p>
    <p>
       Founded in 2025, PIC Lab currently hosts both independently and jointly supervised Master’s and Ph.D. students. We are committed to cultivating an open, collaborative, and interdisciplinary research environment, and we welcome motivated students and researchers to join us in exploring the frontiers of perception and intelligent computing.
    </p>
    <p>
        We sincerely acknowledge the generous support and guidance from many distinguished scholars and collaborators.
    </p>
